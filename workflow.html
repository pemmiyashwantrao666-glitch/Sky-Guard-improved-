Need help?

This form lets you report sensor anomalies you've observed in the field. Our AI will cross-check your observations against baseline data from neighbouring stations.

  • Temperature above 60°C or below -10°C is automatically flagged
  • Frozen values are detected when a reading stays unchanged for 8+ samples
  • Communication errors appear as missing or NaN values
  • Pressure drops without wind/rain response are physics-conflict anomalies
