How it works

From sensor to insight,
in under three seconds

Every weather reading passes through our five-layer detection stack before it reaches your forecast model — catching faults that would otherwise silently corrupt your data.

01

Ingest & Validate

AWS readings stream in via MQTT or HTTP. Layer 1 checks physical plausibility — range limits, communication errors, and missing data are flagged immediately.

02

Learn & Compare

Layers 2 and 3 learn each station's normal temporal and seasonal patterns, then cross-check against neighbouring stations — catching drift, spikes, and outliers.

03

Explain & Act

Layer 4 checks multivariate physics. Layer 5 produces explainable AI output — confidence scores, root-cause labels, corrected values, and maintenance recommendations.
